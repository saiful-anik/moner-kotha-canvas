# Brag Plan: Moner Kotha Canvas

## What is this app?

Moner Kotha is an infinite, hand-drawn wall where people can anonymously leave the small good and bad thoughts on their mind.

## The angle

Not every thought needs an account, a reaction, or a reply. This is a gentle product film for a digital sheet of paper that gets fuller every time someone leaves a line behind.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)

On a nearly empty dotted-paper wall, one handwritten thought lands: “some thoughts just need somewhere to go.”

## Key moments (the middle)

- The camera opens onto a quiet, scattered wall of real Bengali/English thought copy.
- A user selects a mood, types “maybe tomorrow will be better,” and presses `put it here ↗`.
- The new thought settles into the canvas alongside other anonymous lines; the wall becomes a little more human.

## Outro / punchline

“leave it here.” Then: Moner Kotha — no accounts. no likes. no replies.

## User flow worth showing

Enter the infinite canvas → choose good or bad and write a thought → post it as a small, permanent piece of the wall.

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a warm, handcrafted quiet-product film
- Interpretation: restrained motion, long readable holds, tactile paper details, and sincere copy instead of startup language.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: `#fdfbf7`
- Accent: `#2d5da1`, `#ff4d4d`, and post-it `#fff9c4`
- Text: `#2d2d2d`
- Display font: Kalam
- Body font: Patrick Hand
- Strongest visual element: the dotted-paper infinite canvas with scattered, rotated handwritten thoughts and its wobbly composer.

## Share copy (draft)

Made Moner Kotha — an infinite little wall for the thoughts you don’t need to explain. No accounts, no likes, no replies; just leave it here.

## Audio direction

- Role: warm bed with sparse, motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, steady and clean at low volume
- Music treatment: fade in gently; begin fading below the final held wordmark
- Music cue guidance: bundled 109.96 BPM preset; land the canvas reveal near 8.74s, the posted-thought payoff near 13.11s, and the outro near 17.47s. The composer interaction uses the 10.93–13.11s beat window.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let paper warmth and the new thought’s presence breathe slightly, with no visualizer treatment.
- SFX posture: sparse; a soft placement, a quiet button tap, and a final bell only.
- Audio-coupled moments: thought landing, simulated post button press, final wordmark.
- Restraint rule: never make the sound busier or more cheerful than the quiet emotional premise.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — A place to put it — 4s

An empty warm dotted paper wall. One thought writes in, then settles: “some thoughts just need somewhere to go.”

Sequential/interaction: one line arrives and holds for 1.7 seconds.
Audio intent: a soft opening with one gentle placement cue.
Audio-coupled idea: thought landing.
Music: warm bed begins quietly.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — The wall — 5s

The camera reveals a spread of actual product-style thoughts: “ajke mon ta bhalo,” “i miss someone i shouldn’t,” “finally something went right,” and “tired of everything.” Small blue/red scribbles make the wall feel lived in.

Sequential/interaction: four lines settle one by one, then hold as one canvas.
Audio intent: a calm sense of accumulated humanity.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle beat-aligned thought arrivals, with no SFX for every item.
Music: continue with canvas reveal close to 8.74s.
Transition mood: gentle zoom → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Put it here — 6s

Recreate the working composer: select the blue good mood, type “maybe tomorrow will be better,” then click the hand-drawn `put it here ↗` button. The thought lifts from the composer into the wall.

Sequential/interaction: simulated mood selection, typing, button press, then a single posted thought settles and holds.
Audio intent: tactile and reassuring.
Audio-coupled idea: quiet click at mood selection, soft drop at placement; payoff close to 13.11s.
Music: steady beneath the interaction.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Leave it here — 5s

The completed wall holds. Center copy reads “leave it here.” then resolves to `Moner Kotha` and “no accounts. no likes. no replies.”

Sequential/interaction: line one resolves, then the wordmark and promise arrive; final copy holds for 1.5 seconds.
Audio intent: warm resolution with a restrained final bell.
Audio-coupled idea: final wordmark arrives near 17.47s.
Music: fade under the final held frame.
Transition mood: none — settled poster frame.

**Music mood for this video:** warm, steady, clean.

**Audio summary:** A quiet bed and three tactile accents support a handwritten thought becoming part of a shared wall.
